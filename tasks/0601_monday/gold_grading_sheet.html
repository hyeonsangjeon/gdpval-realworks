채점 가이드

This criterion asks whether the deliverable's overall formatting and style are professional and readable. Grade the visible form, not whether the substantive content is correct.

Use the seven dimensions as a decomposition rule: render integrity, layout consistency, readability, visual hierarchy, table legibility, professional finish, and whether a blocking defect is present. Blocking defects include broken layout, unreadable text, blank/default dumps, missing requested deliverable form, or a render/view that prevents judging the style.

If most dimensions are sound and no blocking defect is present, score toward pass. If a blocking defect is present, score toward fail. Mixed quality should receive partial credit. Machine scores are shown only as context; owner_score and owner_reason are the gold source.
